"Vertically integrated" is the kind of phrase that sounds like it was invented in a boardroom. So let me put it in plain terms.

When you sell a house to most investors in the Central Valley, four or five separate companies touch that transaction. The person who found you. The investor who actually funds it. A hard money lender behind that investor. A general contractor who bids the rehab. A listing agent at the end. Every one of them has their own margin, their own timeline, and their own reason to renegotiate.

Ulloa Investment Group is all of those companies. One team, one process, complete control from acquisition to sale.

That is not a marketing line. It is an operating structure, and it changes what we can promise you.

## Acquire, renovate, sell, repeat

That is the whole cycle, and every stage of it happens inside the same company.

Here is what that means in practice on a house we buy in Visalia.

**Day one.** You call. I ask about the property and your timeline. If it is a fit, someone from our team walks it, usually within a day or two. That walkthrough is a contractor walk, not an inspection with a contingency attached.

**Day two or three.** You get an offer built from four numbers: after-repair value from closed comps in your neighborhood, rehab cost from our own construction estimate, holding and closing costs, and margin. I will walk you through each one. I broke that math down in detail in [how to sell a house fast in Visalia without listing it](/blog/selling-a-house-fast-in-visalia).

**You accept.** Escrow opens with a local Tulare County title company. Earnest money goes hard, because we are not shopping your contract to anyone. There is nobody to shop it to. We are the buyer.

**Close in as little as 15 days.** Not because we are heroes about it, but because there is no lender in the transaction. Our own capital funds it. The only thing setting the pace is title work and when you want to be out.

**Then the part you never see.** Our crews take the house. Our brokerage lists it. Nobody outside the company touches that property from the day you sign to the day the next family moves in.

## Why the structure protects you, not just us

Cutting out middlemen sounds like it only benefits the buyer. Here is the part that lands on your side of the table.

**Our offer holds.** The number one complaint about cash buyers is the renegotiation. They offer high, get you under contract, "discover" problems during inspection, and come back 15 grand lower a week before close. That works because they never actually priced the rehab, they just guessed. Our number comes from a construction company that will be doing the work. There is nothing left to discover.

**There is no financing to collapse.** Ulloa funds every deal in-house. No bank, no hard money lender, no outside investor who can get cold feet at the last minute.

**You are not talking to a wholesaler.** Ninety percent of the people texting Visalia homeowners about their property are middlemen who lock up a contract and shop it. If they cannot find a real buyer, they cancel and you lost a month. We acquire, renovate, and resell. That is the entire business.

**We can show you both numbers.** This is the Legacy piece. Because we own a brokerage, I can pull what your house would realistically list for and what we will pay off-market in the same conversation, from the same data. If listing nets you more money, I will tell you so and hand you to our agents. I would rather lose the deal than talk you into the wrong one.
