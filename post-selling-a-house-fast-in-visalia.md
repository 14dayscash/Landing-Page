Most people in Visalia who need to sell quickly assume there are only two
options: list with an agent and wait, or take whatever a national "we buy
houses" outfit offers over the phone. There is a third one, and it is the one I
run every week.

## What an off-market sale actually is

An off-market sale means the house never hits the MLS. No sign in the yard, no
open houses, no strangers walking through your kitchen on a Saturday. You talk
to a buyer directly, agree on a number, and close on a date you pick.

The tradeoff is real and worth stating plainly: you will not get full retail
price. A buyer taking the house as-is is absorbing the repairs, the holding
costs, and the risk that the rehab runs over. That discount is what you are
trading for speed and certainty.

## How the number gets built

There is no mystery to it. Every offer I make comes from the same arithmetic:

1. **After-repair value.** What the house sells for once it is fixed, based on
   what comparable finished homes in that Visalia neighborhood actually closed
   at in the last few months.
2. **Rehab cost.** What it takes to get it there. Roof, HVAC, kitchen, flooring,
   whatever the house needs.
3. **Holding and closing costs.** Taxes, insurance, utilities, and commissions
   on the back end.
4. **Margin.** What is left has to justify the risk, or the deal does not pencil.

Work backward from the after-repair value and you land on the offer. If a buyer
will not walk you through those four numbers, that tells you something.

## When you should list instead

I will say this to a seller's face, and it costs me deals. If your house is in
good shape, you are not on a deadline, and you can handle showings, **list it.**
You will net more. An off-market sale makes sense when at least one of these is
true:

- The house needs work you cannot or do not want to fund
- You are dealing with probate, a divorce, or liens
- You have a deadline that a 45-day escrow will not meet
- It is a rental with tenants and you are done being a landlord

## What closing actually looks like

Walkthrough, then an offer within a day or two. If you accept, it goes to a
local title company and typically closes in two to three weeks. Faster if you
need it. You pick the date, you leave what you do not want, and you do not
repair anything.

That is the whole process. No repairs, no commissions, no financing falling
through a week before close.
