## What an off-market home sale actually means

An off-market sale means your house never hits the MLS. No sign in the yard, no open houses, no strangers walking through your kitchen on a Saturday morning, no repair requests from a buyer's inspector three weeks in.

You talk to a buyer directly, you agree on a number, and you close on a date you pick.

The tradeoff is real and I will state it plainly: you will not get full retail price. A buyer taking the house as-is is absorbing the repairs, the holding costs, the carrying risk, and the chance that the rehab runs over budget. That discount is what you are trading for speed and certainty.

Anybody who tells you that you can have retail price AND a two-week close with zero repairs is selling you something.

## How we build the number on your house

There is no mystery to it. Every cash offer House Junkies makes comes from the same four-part arithmetic, and I will walk any seller through it line by line.

**1. After-repair value (ARV).** What the house sells for once it is fully renovated, based on what comparable finished homes in your specific Visalia neighborhood actually closed at in the last few months. Not Zestimates. Closed comps.

**2. Rehab cost.** What it takes to get it there. Roof, HVAC, electrical, kitchen, flooring, whatever the house needs. Because House Junkies Construction is in-house, our rehab number is a real construction estimate, not a padded guess with a cushion built in to protect a subcontractor bid.

**3. Holding and closing costs.** Property taxes, insurance, utilities, and the commissions we pay on the back end when we resell it.

**4. Margin.** What is left has to justify the risk, or the deal does not pencil and I will tell you so.

Work backward from the after-repair value, subtract rehab, subtract holding and closing, subtract margin, and you land on the offer.

If a buyer will not walk you through those four numbers, that tells you everything you need to know about the offer.

## When you should list your Visalia house instead

I say this to sellers' faces and it costs me deals. If your house is in good shape, you are not on a deadline, and you can handle showings, list it. You will net more money. We will even do it for you through Legacy Real Estate, and I would rather do that than talk you into the wrong sale.

An off-market cash sale is the right move when at least one of these is true:

- The house needs work you cannot fund or do not want to manage
- You are dealing with probate, an estate, a trust sale, a divorce, or liens
- You are behind on payments or facing a foreclosure timeline
- You have a deadline that a 45-day escrow will not meet
- It is a rental with tenants in place and you are done being a landlord
- You inherited a property out of the area and do not want to fly back to manage a listing
- You need to pick your own move-out date instead of having it dictated by a buyer's lender
