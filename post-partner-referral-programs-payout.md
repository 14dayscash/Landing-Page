House Junkies has paid out more than $1,000,000 to people who never worked a day for the company. Some of them just knew about a house that needed to sell and made one phone call. Others put their own money into a deal alongside us and shared in what it made. That kind of payout volume is part of why SFR Analytics ranked us [the #1 investor in Visalia by transaction volume](/blog/sfr-analytics-top-visalia-investors-2026), we close enough deals that referrals and partnerships add up fast. I want to walk through every single way to get paid working with House Junkies, because there is a real chance one of these fits you right now, whether you have an address, a license, or capital sitting around doing nothing.

## Got an Address? That's Worth $2,000 to $10,000 on Its Own

This is the easiest one and it costs you nothing to try. You do not need a real estate license, capital, or a contract of your own.

If you know about a property, a distressed house, a landlord who mentioned being done with tenants, a family member's inherited place, you send us the address and whatever contact information you have. We evaluate it the same way we evaluate every acquisition. If we are able to act on it and the deal closes, you get paid, typically between $2,000 and $10,000 depending on the size and margin of the deal, in cash, check, or wire transfer, usually the day after closing.

That is a one-time referral. Nothing after the intro, no ongoing involvement, and no downside if it does not pan out.

## Got a License? Represent Us Directly

If you hold a California real estate license, you do not have to just refer us a deal, you can represent House Junkies directly on the transaction. That means working the deal itself and earning agent-level compensation, typically $5,000 to $15,000 or more, instead of a flat referral fee. This is the tier for licensed agents who want repeat, reliable volume from an active buyer instead of chasing one-off listings.

## Found a Deal and Have Some Capital? Put It to Work as a JV Partner

This is a step past a referral. You bring the property and put capital toward the acquisition itself, typically $20,000 to $50,000 or more depending on the deal. House Junkies covers 100% of the [construction costs](/blog/california-adu-laws-2026-tulare-county), and if you are a licensed agent, you can also list the finished property on the back end for your commission. Instead of a flat fee for the intro, you are sharing in that specific deal's actual upside.

## Have Capital and Want a Stake Without Running the Deal? Equity Partner

You bring capital as an equal partner on a specific property, generally $35,000 to $100,000 or more. House Junkies runs the entire operation, acquisitions, construction, and the sale through [Legacy Real Estate](/blog/vertical-integration-real-estate-visalia), and the profit splits 50/50 once it sells. You are funding half of it and sharing half of what it makes, without managing any of the moving pieces yourself.

## Want Your Capital Working With Zero Involvement? Funding Partner

The most passive way in. You are not finding deals or touching construction at all. You are providing capital against active flips as a lender, starting around $100,000 deployed per year, and earning an interest-only, double-digit return while your money is working, with terms negotiated individually per agreement. Built for people with capital sitting in low-return accounts who want real estate-backed returns without lifting a finger operationally.

## Where the $1,000,000+ Actually Comes From

That figure is the combined total across every tier, Deal Finder payouts to people who sent us an address and nothing more, agent commissions to licensed partners who represented us on transactions, and profit splits paid out to JV and Equity partners once their deals sold. It is not one program, it is the sum of every one of these working the way they are supposed to.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the actual difference between a referral and a partnership?**
A referral, Deal Finder, is a one-time payout for pointing us at a property, with no further involvement. A partnership means you are bringing capital, a license, or both into the actual deal, and your payout is tied to how that specific transaction performs.

**Do I need a real estate license for any of these?**
Only for Agent Partner. Deal Finder, JV, Equity, and Funding Partner do not require a license, though a license does open up additional opportunities within a JV deal.

**How fast does a Deal Finder referral actually get paid?**
Once the deal you sent us closes, typically the day after, by cash, check, or wire transfer.

**Is this open to people outside Visalia or Tulare County?**
Deal Finder works best with local knowledge of the area, but Equity and Funding Partner arrangements are open to outside capital regardless of where you live.

**What happens if a JV or Equity deal underperforms?**
Real estate carries real risk, including the risk of loss. Every JV and Equity arrangement is documented in a written agreement that spells out how costs, delays, and outcomes are handled before either side commits capital.

**Is there a minimum for Funding Partner?**
These arrangements generally start around $100,000 deployed per year, with interest-only terms negotiated individually per agreement.

Partnership terms, including Equity Partner and Funding Partner arrangements, are negotiated individually and documented in a separate written agreement. The ranges above are general and not a specific offer. Real estate investing carries risk, including the risk of loss, and past performance does not guarantee future results.
