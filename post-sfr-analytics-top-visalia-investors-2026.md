## Where This Number Comes From

SFR Analytics builds this ranking from verified deed and transaction records, not self-reported figures or marketing claims. A few things worth understanding about how a list like this works:

- **It is a snapshot, not a final total.** Deeds record continuously and new transactions close every week, so a ranking published on September 10 reflects activity through that date, not a full-year figure.
- **It is non-exhaustive by nature.** Entity names can be filed or spelled differently across records, and some transactions close under affiliated entities that are not immediately obvious from the paperwork. SFR Analytics accounts for that in how it frames the list, and so should anyone reading it.
- **It is still the most objective read available.** A self-reported "top flipper" claim is easy to make and hard to verify. A ranking built from actual recorded deeds is neither.

That third point is the whole reason this post exists. Anyone can put "#1 in Visalia" on a business card. Not everyone can point to an independent firm that pulled the county's own records and landed on the same conclusion.
